Notes

  • Leads, new leads, purchases and revenue come from Hyros (last click, all sources) for both campaigns' ads (ABO and CBO). Spend, impressions and link clicks come from Meta.
  • Link clicks are Meta's link click metric, not all clicks. Cost per link click is spend divided by link clicks.
  • New leads are people Hyros had not seen before. Leads also counts existing contacts who registered through these ads.
  • Meta pixel leads are lower than Hyros and shown only for comparison.
  • Dates follow the ad account's Pacific time zone. Today is a partial day. Ad level figures can trail campaign totals slightly because Meta updates them at different times.
  • Local copy: run Sewing Dashboard.command (http://localhost:7790) to get a Pull & publish button that pulls Meta and Hyros right now and pushes the new numbers to GitHub.
  • How updating works: a GitHub job re-pulls Meta and Hyros every hour and publishes data.json. This page re-reads it every 5 minutes and when you return to the tab. Refresh now fetches the latest published numbers immediately; it cannot force a new Meta/Hyros pull from this public page, so use "Run pull now on GitHub" for that (needs repo access).
